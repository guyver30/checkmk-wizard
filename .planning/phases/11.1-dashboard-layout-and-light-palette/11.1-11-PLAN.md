<objective>
Close the phase: add the periodic clock that makes staleness visible when the poller goes silent,
prove against React's real re-render behavior that a stale flip is visual-only (D-34), write the
docs D-37 and D-41/D-42 require, and take a human verification pass.

Purpose: the one moment staleness matters is the one moment guaranteed to produce no new MQTT
message, so a clock is required. In the vanilla dashboard that clock turned three full rebuilds
into a 12-second event, destroying scroll and focus. D-43 is explicit that React's reconciler makes
this less of a manual concern but does NOT make it automatic — "must be verified against React's
actual re-render behavior, not assumed away by the framework" is the task here.
Output: `useNowTick`, a dedicated stale-stability regression file, `dashboard-react/README.md`,
deployment-doc updates, and a human-verified parity pass.
</objective>
