2. **D-27 reversed for host-DOWN** (operator, demo requirement): a DOWN host with an open incident also gets its host-level need (tier from criticality). Service/TCP failure needs for covered hosts stay suppressed.
3. **D-24 reversed** (operator): triage is no longer edit-mode-only; it works in normal view and in `?admin=1` (quick 261005-e7b). Needs merged into the Incidents pane as tabs ("Incidents & needs").

Constraint amendment added to CLAUDE.md and PROJECT.md. No auth beyond the dashboard's own (closed network).

## Operational findings
- **The dev/deploy VM needs more RAM.** At 1.9 GB total ClickHouse failed every write with `MEMORY_LIMIT_EXCEEDED` (tracked ~690 MiB against a 407-599 MiB ceiling, RSS only ~130 MiB). The cause of the ~560 MiB of non-resident tracked memory was not identified (the 2 GiB filesystem cache is the only 14.2 ClickHouse config change, unproven). The operator raised the VM memory and the errors stopped (poller `MEMORY_LIMIT` count 0, available 2385 MB). Recommend at least 3-4 GB for the full stack.
- **Misleading analytics warning**: when ClickHouse is unreachable at start-up, analytics reports `Analytics tables missing [events, incidents, need_triage]: run ...14.2-analytics.sql`, although the real cause is "connection refused". Not fixed.
- **Silent cycles**: the plan expected per-metric series counts in the analytics log; a healthy cycle logs nothing at INFO. Not fixed.
- A stopped systemd service on an UP host will only produce a need once Checkmk reports it CRIT; with the 15 s tick this happens within about a minute. The operator verified needs through faked DOWN hosts.
- The stack was started by a foreground `podman compose up` from the operator's terminal after `init-env.sh`; `init-env.sh` in the repository does not launch compose and the cause of that launch was not found. Ctrl-C in that terminal would stop the stack.
