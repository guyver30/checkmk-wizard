## Decisions

- Open-row change detection ignores `since` (it is the open time, not content); content tuple is root, root_state, inferred, confirmed_down, not_observable, dependents, worst_criticality. Seeded rows therefore are not rewritten when the same incident is re-delivered after restart.
- Closed rows reuse the last known incident dict; reconcile uses the seeded row's worst_criticality for the summary.
- Control characters stripped from note/by include C1 range (0x7f-0x9f).
