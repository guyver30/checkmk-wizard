## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-11-41 | Information Disclosure | undocumented credential exposure | mitigate | Task 1 requires the endpoint and credentials section to state plainly that the `wsreader` credentials are readable from `dashboard/js/config.js` by anyone who can load the page, together with the mitigation (`topic read lan/#` only) and the rotation path (`deploy/gen-mosquitto-passwd.sh`). An operator who does not know about an exposure cannot decide about it; silence here would be the actual vulnerability |
| T-11-42 | Spoofing | a dashboard trusted while showing stale data | mitigate | Checkpoint steps 14 through 16 prove the poller-offline banner, the global hatch and their recovery against a real stopped poller, and step 15 proves stale is visually distinct from DOWN. A dashboard that silently shows old data as current is the single worst failure mode for a monitoring tool, and this is the only check that proves it does not |
| T-11-43 | Denial of Service | synchronized reconnect storm against the broker | mitigate | Checkpoint step 18 verifies with two concurrent tabs that the reconnect delay grows and differs between clients. A fixed identical interval would mean the built-in mqtt.js retry is still active and every open kiosk tab would hammer mosquitto in lockstep after a restart; this step is what distinguishes the two outcomes observationally |
| T-11-44 | Tampering | forged or malformed payload reaching the DOM | mitigate | The `textContent`-only controls are enforced by acceptance criteria in plans 11-07, 11-08 and 11-09. Checkpoint step 20 adds live confirmation that no page throws during real rendering of real Checkmk-sourced strings |
| T-11-45 | Repudiation | success asserted without evidence | mitigate | The checkpoint requires the observed reconnect sequence and the retained payload keys to be written out in the summary rather than summarised as "works". Failed criteria are recorded as gaps for `/bm:plan-phase --gaps` instead of being patched inside a verification step |
| T-11-SC | Tampering | npm/pip/cargo installs | mitigate | No package installs in this plan. The only third-party artifact in the phase, `mqtt` 5.15.2, was audited (`slopcheck` verdict `[OK]`) and hash-recorded in plan 11-02, and Task 1 documents that pin for the operator |
