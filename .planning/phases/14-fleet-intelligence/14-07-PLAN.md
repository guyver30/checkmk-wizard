<objective>
Implement the label-carriage half of PLR-16: read operator-set criticality, per-service criticality and depends-on links from Checkmk host labels (D-07, D-08, D-09) and carry them to every viewer on `lan/devices/topology`, feeding the worst-affected-criticality closure plan 14-01 already implemented.

Purpose: business knowledge Checkmk does not have (D-05) becomes visible to every viewer without the browser holding a read credential.
Output: poller label constants, strict parsers, and topology node/signature/normalisation extensions, with flat tests.

Label design (D-09, Claude's discretion from 14-RESEARCH.md "Label Design", adopted as-is; 14-RESEARCH.md A1):
- `criticality:<tier>` with tier in `low|medium|high|critical`; absent/invalid → `low`.
- `service_criticality:<name>=<tier>;<name>=<tier>` — one label holding every per-service override for the host (D-08, keyed by service name).
- `depends_on:<host>,<host>` on the dependent host (e.g. the screen), pointing at what it depends on (e.g. the media server).
Checkmk labels allow any character except `:` in key and value (docs.checkmk.com/latest/en/labels.html, cited in 14-RESEARCH.md); all delimiters above are colon-free.
</objective>
