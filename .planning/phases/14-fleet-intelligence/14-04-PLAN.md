<objective>
Implement DASH-14's incident card list and wire DASH-15 dimming to live data, in the non-kiosk overview.

Purpose: operators see causes, not N alarms (D-12), with evidence-respecting wording (D-04).
Output: `IncidentCard`, `IncidentList`, and `IndexRoute` wiring.

Decisions settled here (UI-SPEC checker recommendations):
- **Primary focal point of the non-kiosk screen:** when one or more incidents are open, the incident list (top of the centre pane, first thing read) is the focal point; when none are open it collapses to one quiet line and the topology map is the focal point. To keep the map usable, the list is capped at `max-h-[35vh]` with `overflow-y-auto` in the non-kiosk layout.
- Card status mapping uses `incidentStatus()` from lib/incidents.ts (danger only with confirmed-down evidence or a non-inferred DOWN root; otherwise warning).
- The expanded "View devices" section lists confirmed-down and not-observable hosts (each a link to /details) and, when present, "Dependent devices: …" — the extra line explains a worst criticality that comes from a dependent host (PLR-16 closure), and adds no status claim.
</objective>
