<objective>
Implement DASH-15: in the fleet tree and on the topology map, consequence hosts of an open incident stay visible but dimmed and point to their incident instead of raising their own alarm; only the root keeps alarm styling (D-12, UI-SPEC "Dimmed Consequence Treatment").

Purpose: one switch failure reads as one alarm, not seven.
Output: additive, optional `incidentLookup` input on the pure tree/map model builders and components. Wiring the lookup from the store into IndexRoute is plan 14-04 (keeps this plan free of IndexRoute.tsx so the dependency chain stays linear).

Decisions recorded here (Claude's discretion, UI-SPEC-consistent):
- Inferred-root styling: warning hue (`STATE_HEX.WARN` #f97316) with a dashed border on the map, and a `Badge color="warning" variant="soft"` reading "Inferred, not confirmed" in the tree. Rationale: the inferred root's own Checkmk state is UP (it is unchecked), so reusing DOWN/UNREACH red would assert something monitoring cannot see (D-04); warning matches the incident card's "warning" status for inferred incidents (UI-SPEC status rule).
- Group roll-up accents and the stats strip are unchanged: they are counts-by-state (DASH-01), not per-device alarms. DASH-15 applies at the device row / map node level.
- Map click on a consequence node navigates to `/?incident={id}` (DASH-15 "links to its incident"); the tree keeps the device-name link to /details plus a separate "See incident" link, so the details page stays reachable from the tree.
</objective>
