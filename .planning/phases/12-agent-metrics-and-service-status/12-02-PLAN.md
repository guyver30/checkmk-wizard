<objective>
Turn `ServiceSnapshot` rows into the three published shapes Phase 12's dashboard consumes: gauge
fields on the existing status topic (D-12), a change-only per-device service list on
`lan/devices/{id}/services` (D-11/D-13), and a bounded per-service transition history on
`lan/devices/{id}/service_history` (D-14).

Purpose: settles ROADMAP Phase 12 scope item 5 (publish cadence — ~21 hosts × ~20 services is far
too many rows to republish every cycle) by splitting volatile gauge numbers onto the already
every-cycle status topic and keeping the row list change-only.
Output: new topics, publishers and cycle wiring in `scripts/mqtt_poller.py`, with tests.
</objective>
