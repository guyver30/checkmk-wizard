<objective>
Daily availability rollups: compute each local day's figures from ClickHouse's state table, write them as JSON (boto3, never-overwrite) and Parquet (ClickHouse s3() export of the identical rows), backfill missing days on startup and after midnight.

Purpose: HIST-06/07/07a (D-45..D-53, D-57). JSON feeds future dashboard views; Parquet is the Databricks/Athena bridge.
Output: rollup functions, scheduler and wiring in scripts/mqtt_poller.py; tests; boto3/tzdata dependencies.
</objective>
