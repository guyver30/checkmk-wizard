| T-14.1-09 | Tampering | /availability/ → MinIO writes/deletes | mitigate | `limit_except GET { deny all; }` on every forwarded location; strict object-key regex; bucket policy grants anonymous s3:GetObject only (plan 14.1-04); smoke check_write_methods_rejected and check_bucket_anonymous_readonly |
