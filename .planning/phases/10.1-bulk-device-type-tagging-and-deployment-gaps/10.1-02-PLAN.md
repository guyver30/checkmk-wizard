<objective>
Close the four deployment and observability gaps Phase 10's own live verification run recorded as findings 2-5 (`.planning/phases/10-checkmk-tag-group-onboarding-integration/10-06-SUMMARY.md`): the `worker` service's missing REST credentials (OPS-01), the never-displayed automation secret (OPS-02), the poller's zero-tolerance Livestatus startup failure (OPS-03), and its silent successful start (OPS-04).

Purpose: each of these cost real operator time during the Phase 10 verification run. Finding 4 in particular was observed live — a routine Checkmk container restart raced the poller and killed it, and only `restart: unless-stopped` recovered it. These are four small, surgical, independently-verifiable fixes at four already-identified sites; none needs a new abstraction and none introduces a dependency.

Output: a worker service that can authenticate to Checkmk, a wizard that shows the operator the secret it just generated, and a poller that survives a container-restart race and says so when it starts.

**Scope guard (read before touching `run_forever`):** OPS-03's requirement wording ("one consistent failure posture") must NOT be implemented as literal symmetry between Livestatus and REST. See Task 2 — the asymmetry is a deliberate Phase 10 decision (D-03), the real defect is the absence of retry, and the fix must say so in-source.
</objective>
