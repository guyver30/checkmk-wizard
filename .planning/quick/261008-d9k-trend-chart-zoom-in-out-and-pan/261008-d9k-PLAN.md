  <behavior>
    - clampDomain: a domain wider than full returns full; narrower than the minimum span widens to the minimum span around its centre; a domain sticking out on the left or right shifts back inside full keeping its span; when full itself is shorter than MIN_SPAN_MS the minimum is full's span (never wider than full).
    - zoomAt(d, anchorMs, factor, full): factor 0.5 halves the span and keeps the anchor at the same fraction of the window (anchor at 25% of d stays at 25% of the result, unless clamping shifts it); factor 2 doubles; zooming out repeatedly ends at exactly full; zooming in repeatedly stops at MIN_SPAN_MS; an anchor outside d is clamped into d first.
    - panBy(d, deltaMs, full): shifts both ends by deltaMs, keeps the span, stops at either edge of full (panning past the right edge leaves d[1] === full[1]).
    - revealTime(d, ms, full): returns d unchanged when ms is inside; otherwise shifts (same span) so ms sits 10% of the span inside the nearer edge, then clamps.
    - isFullDomain(d, full) true only when both ends are within 1 ms of full; isMinSpan(d, full) true when the span is within 1 ms of the effective minimum.
  </behavior>

- `clampDomain(d, full): Domain`, `zoomAt(d, anchorMs, factor, full): Domain`, `panBy(d, deltaMs, full): Domain`, `revealTime(d, ms, full): Domain`, `isFullDomain(d, full): boolean`, `isMinSpan(d, full): boolean` with the behaviour above. `zoomAt` computes new start as anchor - (anchor - d[0]) * factor and new end as anchor + (d[1] - anchor) * factor, then `clampDomain`.
